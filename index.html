What is Pop It?

A Pop It is a silicone sensory toy made up of flexible bubbles that can be pressed from one side to the other. It mimics the satisfying sensation of popping bubble wrap, providing an effective way to relieve stress, improve concentration, and regulate emotions.

Game Rules

Players take turns pressing one or more bubbles in a single row. The player who is forced to press the last bubble loses the game. Once all the bubbles have been pressed, simply flip the Pop It over and start again!
